## 熵:表示不确定度  
$$
H(x) = \sum_{x\in \mathscr{X}}P(X)\log{\frac{1}{P(X)}}
$$

**Def** 两个概率分布的距离  
设 $p, q$为两个$r.v.$，定义$q,p$的距离  
$$
V(p, q) = \sum_{x \in \mathscr{X}}|p(x)-q(x)| 
$$

**Def** 熵的收敛  
H(P) 在随机变量距离下收敛到一个$P_{0} \in \mathscr{P}_{\mathscr{X}}$，如果$\forall \epsilon >0, \exists \delta>0$当$\forall q$分布，$V(P_{0},q)<\delta$时，使得  
$$
|H(q)-H(P_{0})|<\epsilon
$$  
记为  
$$
\lim_{ q \to P_{0} }H(q) = H(P_{0}) 
$$  

**Def**  
$$
h(n) = \left\{\begin{matrix}
H\left(  \left\{ \frac{1}{n}, \frac{1}{n}, \dots, \frac{1}{n}  \right\} \right) \\
H(\{ 0, 1 \})
\end{matrix}
\right.
$$

熵三个公理  

### 公理一
熵有对称性和连续性  
- 对称性
$$
H(\{ p, 1-p \}) = H(\{ 1-p, p \})
$$  
- 连续性 
$$
\lim_{ q \to P_{0} }H(q) = H(P_{0}) = H(\lim_{ q \to P_{0} }q)
$$  

### 公理二  
$h(n)$关于$n$单调不减  

### 公理三 (分组性质)  
将$X$分为$k$个不变的非空组$G_{1}, G_{2}, \dots, G_{k}$， 即  
$$
X = \cup_{i = 1}^k G_{i}
$$  
分别对应概率$P_{1}, P_{2}, .., P_{k} \geq 0$,即有  
$$
H(P) = H({P_{1}, P_{2}, .., P_{k}}) + \sum_{i=1}^k P_{i}H\left( \left\{  \frac{P(g)}{P_{i}}, g\in G_{i}  \right\} \right)
$$  

**引理1**  
$$
\forall m, n \in \mathbb{Z}^+, h(mn) = h(m) + h(n)
$$

**推论1**  
$$
h(\Pi_{i=1}^kn_{i}) = \sum_{i=1}^k h(n_{i})
$$

**推论2**  
$$
h(2^k) = kh(2) = k, \forall k \in \mathbb{Z}^+
$$  

**引理2**  
$$
\forall n \in \mathbb{Z}^+, h(n) = \log n
$$  

于是由上述三条公理可推出熵的形式为  
$$
H(x) = \sum_{x\in \mathscr{X}}P(X)\log{\frac{1}{P(X)}}
$$  

## 相对熵  

### 联合熵  
**Def**  
$$
H(X, Y) = \sum_{(X,Y)\in \mathscr{X \times Y}}P(X, Y)\log \frac{1}{P(X, Y)}
$$  
类似可定义$n$维向量联合熵  

### 自信息  
$$
i(X) = -\log P(X)
$$  
则  
$$
H(X) = E i(X)
$$  
类似可定义联合自信息  

### 条件熵  
**Def**  Y的条件熵， X为条件  
$$
H(Y|X) = \sum_{x \in \mathscr{X}}\sum_{Y\in \mathscr{Y}}P(X, Y)\log \frac{1}{P(Y|X)} = \sum_{x\in\mathscr{X}}P(X)H(Y|X=x)
$$  
即对每个固定的$x$求均值  

1. $H(Y)-H(Y|X)=H(X)-H(X|Y)$
2. $H(X,Y) = H(Y|X) + H(X) = H(X|Y)+H(Y)$
3. $i(Y|X) = \log \frac{1}{P(Y|X)}, H(Y|X) = E(i(Y|X))$
4. $H(Y|X)\geq 0$

注意$H(X)=0 \iff P(X+EX)=1$，所以$H(Y|X)=0 \iff Y$是$X$的函数  

## 互信息  

**Def**  
定义$p, q \in \mathscr{P}_{\mathscr{X}}$，则相对熵（又称KL散度）定义为  
$$
D(p||q) = \sum_{X\in \mathscr{X}}p(X)\log \frac{p(x)}{q(x)}
$$

**Remark**  
1. 定义$0\log 0 = 0, 0\log \frac{0}{q} = 0, p\log \frac{p}{0} = \infty$  
2. $D(p||q) \geq 0$，等式成立当仅且当$p=q$
3. $\forall p, \{ q_{k} \}_{k=1}^\infty, q_{k \to p}$依$D$收敛，即$\lim_{ k \to \infty }D(p||q_{k})=0$，则$q_{k}\to p, k\to \infty$依$L 1$收敛  

**Thm**Rinsker不等式  
$$
D(p||q)\geq \frac{1}{2\ln2} V^2(p,q)\geq0
$$

### 条件散度（相对熵）  
$$
D(p(Y|X)||q(Y|X)|p(X)) = \sum_{X \in \mathscr{X}} p(X) D(p(Y|X=x)||q(Y|X=x))=\sum_{x,y}p(X,Y)\log \frac{p(Y|X)}{q(Y|X)}
$$  

**Def** 互信息  
$$
I(X;Y) = D(P(X,Y)||P(X)P(Y)) = \sum_{x\in \mathscr{X}, y\in \mathscr{Y}}P(X,Y) \log \frac{P(X,Y)}{P(X)P(Y)} = H(Y) - H(Y|X)
$$


**Remark**  
1. 意义： $x,y$ 共有的信息量
2. $I(X; Y) = I(Y; X), I(X;X) = H(X)$  
3. $I(X;Y)\geq 0, I(X;Y)=0 \iff X$与$Y$独立
4. $i(X;Y)= \log \frac{P(X,Y)}{P(X)P(Y)}$,则$I(X;Y) = E_{{P_{XY}}}i(X;Y)$
5. $I(X,Y) = E i(X,Y) = H(X)+H(Y)-H(X,Y)$  

**Def** 条件互信息  
$X,Y$的条件互信息，条件为$Z$，定义为  
$$
I(X;Y|Z) = \sum_{z}P(Z=z)I(X;Y |Z=z) = \sum_{z}P(Z=z)D(P(X,Y|Z=z)||P(X|Z=z)P(Y|Z=z))= \sum_{x,y,z}P(X,Y,Z)\log \frac{P(X,Y|Z)}{P(X|Z)P(Y|Z)}
$$

1. $I(X;Y|Z) = H(X|Z)+H(Y|Z)-H(X,Y|Z)=H(X|Z)-H(Y|X,Z)= H(Y|Z)-H(X|Y,Z)$
2. $I(X;Y|Z)\geq 0$，取等$\iff X,Y$关于$Z$条件独立   
3. $I(X;Y|Z) = D(P(X,Y|Z)||P(X|Z)P(Y|Z)|P(Z))$  

## 基本的不等式  
### 熵组  
1. $H(X)\geq 0$
2. $H(X,Y)\geq 0$
3. $H(Y|X)\geq 0$
4. $H(X,Y|Z)\geq 0$

### 散度组  
1. $D(p||q)\geq 0$
2. $D(p||q | R)\geq 0$
3. $I(X;Y)\geq 0$
4. $I(X;Y|Z)\geq 0$

## 链式法则  
### 联合熵  
$$
H(x_{1},x_{2},\dots,x_{n}) = H(x_{1})+H(X_{2}|X_{1}) + \dots + H(x_{n}|x_{1},\dots,x_{n-1})
$$  

### 条件联合熵  
$$
H(x_{1},x_{2},\dots,x_{n}|Y) = H(x_{1}|Y)+H(X_{2}|X_{1},Y) + \dots + H(x_{n}|x_{1},\dots,x_{n-1}, Y)
$$    

### 互信息  
$$
I(X_{1},X_{2},\dots,X_{n};Y) = I(X_{1};Y)+I(X_{2};Y|X_{1})+\dots+I(X_{n};Y|X_{1},\dots,X_{n-1})
$$  

### KL散度  
$$
D(p(X_{1}^n)||q(X_{1}^n)) = \sum_{i}^nD(P(X_{i}|X_{1}^{i-1})||Q(X_{i}|X_{}^{i-1})|P(X_{1}^{i-1}))
$$  

## 不等式  
1. $H(X)\geq H(X|Y)$，取等$\iff X,Y$相互独立
2. $H(X)\geq H(X|Y)\geq H(X|Y,Z)\geq \dots$，即条件不会增加熵  
3. $X\sim \{P(X):x\in \mathscr{X}\}, |\mathscr{X}|< \infty$，那么有$H(X)\leq \log|\mathscr{X}|$，取等$\iff X$是均匀分布  
4. $H(X_{1}^n)\leq \sum_{i=1}^nH(X_{i})$
5. 若$f(x)$是凸函数，则$Ef(X)\geq f(EX)$，如果$f$严格凸，则取等$\iff X$连续或$P(X=EX)=1$
6. $\forall a_{i}^n, b_{i}^n \in \mathbb{R}^*$且$b_{i}$不全为$0$, 有$\sum_{1}^n a_{i}\log \frac{a_{i}}{b_{i}}\geq \left( \sum_{1}^n a_{i} \right)\log \frac{\sum_{1}^n a_{i}}{\sum_{1}^nb_{i}}$，如果$b_{i}=0$，令$\log \frac{a_{i}}{b_{i}}=+\infty$， 取等$\iff \frac{a_i}{b_i}=c$
7. $\forall \lambda\in[0,1], \lambda D(p_{1}||q_{1})+(1-\lambda)D(p_{2}||q_{2})\geq D(\lambda p_{1}+(1-\lambda)p_{2}||\lambda q_{1}+(1-\lambda)q_{2})$
8. $\lambda H(p_{1})+(1-\lambda)H(p_{2})\leq H(\lambda p_{1} + (1-\lambda)p_{2})$
9. $I(X;Y)$是凸的
